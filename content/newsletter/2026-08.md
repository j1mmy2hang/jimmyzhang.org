Dear friends,

I am now writing from my dorm at the University of Michigan Ann Arbor. I arrived here last Wednesday, so it's been one week today. 

Life has started to move in a "slowly hectic" way. 

Schoolwork hasn't really kicked in, but I feel the momentum building. 

Logistics of life like health insurance, banking, driver's license, and other odds and ends are still yet to be fully resolved. 

Deciding on which student clubs to join looms in the horizon, and further out still is the question of research / internships / employment, made murkier by the tightening CPT policies.

On my first day here, I was overwhelmed by how much bigger Ann Arbor felt compared to my usual range in Suzhou or Maastricht. But after walking the campus back and forth several times, I'm starting to feel more grounded here.

Facing a new environment always leaves you feeling a little exposed. Still, I've been fortunate to be welcomed by quite a few kind and supporting people, and I feel more supported by this place and its people.

That said, my friends reveal to me their impression of me — then and now — is still "that nonchalant guy." Maybe they're onto something, and I should throw myself into life with a bit more passion and presence.

Overall, I still felt like time isn't enough for me to truly feel my own living, and I haven't found my rhythm yet. 

There are still dust in the air that are yet to be settled after my journey here.
