## Project "Intention" in Progress! 

The idea is simple — I believe that we should use digital tools with intention. We should *know what we are doing* every time we open our laptop rather than drifting into screen time without awareness.

So here's what _Intention_ does — it's a reminder app that prompts you to state your intention each time you open your laptop. It asks for an estimated time, starts a countdown, and checks in with you when the time is up. Over time, it builds a record of how you've spent your hours, helping you answer the question: _where did your time actually go?_
