If there's one thing that I figured out in Sepetember 2026 is that I won't transfer school again. I am ready to stay in Ann Arbor for 2026 - 2030. 

---

I have never truly felt belonged anywhere. One year in Changshu and two in Maastricht — in neither place did I feel grounded. The environment always seemed to limit me, so I kept looking for an escape, always drafting the next application and hoping somewhere better would accept me.

Gradually I realized that maybe there is no escape. You can't keep eyeing a better place while complaining that you're trapped where you are. 

That is just a pathetic way of living. 

I just want to be here and enjoy every moment, every piece of land, and every person I meet. 

---

There's always this hidden pressure to act fast or fall behind that creeped into my head, probably from social media and other sources: go to office hours, connect with professors, apply to be an RA, get an internship find a job blablabla. 

But damn it I don't feel ready. I don't know enough yet to show a professor anything significant. I just want to learn first.

I assumed college would finally give me the time to actually learn that I claimed I never had in high school no?

Then it occurred to me that it is probably not too late at all. If I'm not transferring, I have four full years to take this slowly and calmly. 

Four years, that is a long period of time if you think about it. 

And that gives me a new level of calm.

---

Together, these two realizations settle it: I won't transfer, and I'll fully enjoy my four years here. 

Besides, who knows what the world will look like in 2030. By then, the things things that I am struggling with now probably won't really matter at all.
