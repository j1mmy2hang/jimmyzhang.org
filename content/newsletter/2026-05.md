Dear friends, 

May is a significant month that marks an important transition of my life. 

First, I have graduated from United World College Maastricht and officially finished high school. Second, I turned 18 on May 28th, 2026.

The first part of May until 22nd has been quite peaceful, filled with IB exams and plenty of free time. 

I wrote about how [[dont-need-computer|most of the times we don't need laptops]], another reflection following April's "[[computer-as-tool|Computer as tool, not space for thinking]]". 

I built two small apps for myself: Life Control Center, a dashboard combining my calendar, reminders, and Notion tasks with a gamification system; and [ObNote](https://github.com/j1mmy2hang/ob-note) for Ipad — a simplified Apple Notes app, but for opening and editing notes in my Obsidian folder, built for fun and aesthetics.

Most importantly, I made and published [an interview with Harry Zhu](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1bpdAB8EVR/), a good friend of mine here. We talked about the UWC life, human connections, university, and more. We might not have talked about something super deep or revealing, but nevertheless, it was a great pleasure as always to talk with Harry, and the audience response was quite positive. More episodes to come in the following months. 

After graduation, things became significantly more chaotic and energetic. Starting from 23rd, I traveled with my parents in Brussels, Paris, and Barcelona. Then, as a small coming-of-age marker, I'll spend 10 days at a [Vipassana meditation retreat](https://www.dhamma.org/en/index) in Barcelona — no devices, no reading, no music, no talking.

I am not expecting some type of true life transformation, but I just hope it could be a mindful opening for my adulthood. 

Until then. 

Sincerely,
Jimmy Zhang
