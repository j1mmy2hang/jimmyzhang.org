Dear friends, 

Life has been great! 

I visited Atlanta, Chicago, and Philly this month, huge thanks to Katherine, Johnny, and Will for all the arrangements and hosting. I feel very grateful to reconnect with CSC friends, many of whom I haven't met for years, yet I can still easily connect to and rely on. 

**A mindset shift:** I rediscovered the value of learning in class. I used to tune out and teach myself afterwards, believing it was more efficient. But once I started actually listening and taking notes, I saw the value that a human teacher brings by guiding you through the learning, in an emobodied form. 

**A self-observation:** I'm overly inclined to systematize everything and reason from first principles. I spend more time designing the perfect framework than doing the thing. Meaningful progress takes less theorizing and more consistent execution. As Nike says, sometimes you need to "just do it" instead of planning and theorzing in the laptop. I have to walk out of the comfort zone of my Obsidian vault — Notes are note the outcomes; action is.
