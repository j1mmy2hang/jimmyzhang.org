## Certainty is a limit, never a state

Every hypothesis keeps some weight; it just gets outmultiplied. A rational mind always leaves a crack for the rival ([[Hold belief & doubt at the same time]]) — which is exactly why rational minds can change  

## Confidence is earned by surviving contact with evidence

Hypotheses that keep predicting what happens keep their weight; surprised ones bleed weight to rivals.
