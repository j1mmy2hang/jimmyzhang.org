Andrej Karpathy and AI systems engineering into a single hour.  
  
His core thesis: "You can actually delete everything… Delete everything, keep Graph."  
  
Here is the entire roadmap broken down:  
  
1\. The Foundation (10%): Treat the LLM as a raw computer you reprogram on the fly.  
2\. The Code (30%): Natural language is your new programming syntax.  
3\. The Agency (50%): Connect memory, tools, and context so predictions trigger execution.  
4\. The Loops (70%): Keep inference-time reasoning strictly separate from weight optimization.  
5\. The Network (100%): Turn complex agent workflows into directed, message-passing graphs.
